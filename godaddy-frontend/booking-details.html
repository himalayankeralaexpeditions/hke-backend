Payment Terms

  • Booking is confirmed only after successful payment receipt.
  • Minimum 20% advance must be collected at the time of booking.
  • The remaining balance must be paid at least 10 days before trip start date.
  • For trips starting in less than 10 days, full payment may be required immediately.
  • Live hotel, vehicle and seasonal rates remain subject to availability until payment confirmation.

Cancellation & Reschedule Policy

  • Advance payment is generally non-refundable after hotel / transport blocking begins.
  • Cancellation more than 15 days before travel may allow rescheduling support subject to supplier rules.
  • Cancellation between 10 to 15 days before travel may involve partial retention charges.
  • Cancellation within 10 days of travel may attract high cancellation charges.
  • No-show or same-day cancellation may lead to 100% retention based on confirmed services.
  • Rescheduling depends on room availability, season difference and transport rebooking conditions.

Important Booking Conditions

  • Displayed amount is an estimated competitive package price based on current trip details and season demand.
  • Actual final price may vary slightly based on final hotel, permits, vehicle availability and live market conditions.
  • Entry fees, activities, airfare/train fare and personal expenses are excluded unless specifically mentioned.
