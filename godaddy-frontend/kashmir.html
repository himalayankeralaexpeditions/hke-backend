Day-wise itinerary

Thoughtfully designed for travelers who want comfort, scenic beauty, and the most loved highlights of Kashmir in one smooth premium journey.

Day 1 – Jammu Arrival • Scenic Transfer to Pahalgam

On arrival at Jammu, our representative will receive you and begin your beautiful journey towards Pahalgam, one of Kashmir’s most loved valley destinations. As the road opens into mountain landscapes, flowing rivers, and refreshing views, the holiday mood begins instantly. After reaching Pahalgam, check in and unwind in the peaceful surroundings. The cool air, calm scenery, and relaxed atmosphere make this the perfect start to your Kashmir escape.

Day 2 – Pahalgam Exploration • Continue to Srinagar

After breakfast, enjoy the charm of Pahalgam and its scenic valley atmosphere. This part of the trip offers some of the most memorable natural beauty in the Kashmir circuit, ideal for couples, families, and photography lovers. Later, proceed towards Srinagar, the heart of Kashmir, known for its timeless beauty, vibrant local culture, and iconic lakeside experiences. On arrival, settle into your stay and enjoy a relaxed evening.

Day 3 – Srinagar Local Sightseeing • Gardens, Views & Classic Kashmir Experience

Today is dedicated to the elegance of Srinagar. Explore the city’s famous sightseeing spots, beautiful garden areas, and scenic viewpoints that make Kashmir one of India’s most sought-after destinations. Depending on your pace and interest, you can also enjoy the classic Srinagar experience with a relaxing shikara ride atmosphere and local market charm. This day adds grace, romance, and cultural depth to your journey.

Day 4 – Full Day Excursion to Gulmarg

After breakfast, proceed for a memorable day trip to Gulmarg, a destination known for its wide meadows, mountain backdrop, and premium holiday feel. The journey itself is scenic, and once you arrive, the beauty of Gulmarg creates one of the strongest highlights of the entire package. Guests can enjoy the fresh mountain environment, open landscapes, and optional activities depending on the season. Return after sightseeing with unforgettable memories.

Day 5 – Sonmarg Excursion • Valley Beauty at Its Best

Today you visit Sonmarg, another stunning gem of Kashmir, admired for its dramatic river views, alpine surroundings, and breathtaking mountain setting. This excursion adds a powerful scenic contrast to your overall itinerary and makes the package feel complete. The drive, the open valley atmosphere, and the peaceful surroundings together create a premium sightseeing day that travelers remember long after the trip ends.

Day 6 – Return Journey to Jammu • Departure with Beautiful Memories

After breakfast, begin your return journey towards Jammu with your heart full of unforgettable Kashmir moments. From valley drives and iconic sightseeing to peaceful stays and mountain charm, this tour is designed to give you a balanced and satisfying holiday experience. Reach Jammu for your onward departure, carrying back not just photographs, but a complete travel story worth repeating.
