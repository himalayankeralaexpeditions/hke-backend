Choose your Leh circuit route

Pick the route that matches your entry point, road preference, weather window, and travel style.

Classic Entry

Srinagar → Leh

A smoother entry into Ladakh with progressive altitude gain, beautiful valley transitions, and a better acclimatization experience for many travellers.

  • Ideal for comfortable entry into Ladakh
  • Common flow: Srinagar → Sonamarg → Kargil → Leh
  • Better for families, mixed-age groups, and relaxed pacing
  • Excellent when you want to continue later toward Nubra and Pangong
Adventure Entry

Manali → Leh

A more adventurous and raw Himalayan entry with dramatic high-altitude landscapes, longer driving stretches, and stronger expedition character.

  • Ideal for adventure-driven road travellers
  • Common flow: Manali → Jispa / Sarchu → Leh
  • Best for expedition feel, road-trip lovers, and bike groups
  • Requires better readiness for tougher road sections
Popular Full Circuit

Srinagar → Leh → Manali

Enter through Srinagar for better acclimatization, explore Ladakh properly, then exit through Manali for a complete and scenic circuit experience.

  • Most balanced premium circuit
  • Combines comfort + adventure in one journey
  • Perfect for travellers wanting both sides of Ladakh
  • Excellent for premium package presentation and sales
Reverse Expedition

Manali → Leh → Srinagar

Start with the raw adventure side and exit from Srinagar. Good for travellers who want an energetic opening and a softer finish.

  • Best for strong road-trip mindset
  • Adventure-first flow
  • Great for photography and riding groups
  • Works well for specific flight or travel logistics
Extended Scenic Route

Manali → Leh → Pangong → Srinagar

A strong premium circuit for travellers who want to enter from Manali, explore Leh and Pangong, then exit from Srinagar for a more complete Ladakh road experience.

  • Adventure entry from Manali side
  • Includes Pangong extension
  • Exit through Srinagar for a balanced finish
  • Excellent for premium road-trip customers
Extended Scenic Route

Srinagar → Leh → Pangong → Manali

A very practical and attractive premium circuit where travellers enter smoothly from Srinagar, cover Leh and Pangong, then exit through Manali for a full Himalayan road journey.

  • Smoother entry from Srinagar side
  • Includes Pangong extension
  • Adventure-style exit via Manali
  • One of the best premium Leh circuit formats
