Day-wise itinerary

A smooth mountain holiday planned to keep every day scenic, comfortable, and worth booking.

Day 1 – Arrival in Manali • Relaxed Check-in & Fresh Mountain Welcome

On arrival, check in and settle into the beautiful mountain atmosphere of Manali. The cool air, river-side energy, and Himalayan setting immediately set the tone for a memorable holiday. Spend the rest of the day at leisure, relax at your hotel, or enjoy a gentle local evening experience depending on your arrival schedule.

Day 2 – Manali Local Sightseeing • Culture, Views & Leisure

After breakfast, enjoy local sightseeing around Manali and explore the charm that makes it one of India’s most popular hill destinations. This day allows you to experience the relaxed side of the town while also enjoying major points of interest, local market character, and beautiful valley surroundings. It is the ideal balance between travel comfort and sightseeing value.

Day 3 – Solang Valley Excursion

Proceed towards Solang Valley, known for its open landscapes, scenic mountain backdrop, and adventure atmosphere. The views here are wide, fresh, and truly memorable. Whether you want to enjoy the natural beauty, click beautiful pictures, or simply spend quality time in the valley environment, this day becomes one of the strongest highlights of the trip.

Day 4 – Rohtang Pass Side / Snow Point Experience

Today is reserved for the much-awaited high-altitude mountain experience depending on permit and seasonal accessibility. This part of the journey gives the holiday its classic Manali feel, with dramatic roads, changing landscapes, and the excitement of reaching snow-view areas. The day adds a strong adventure touch while still keeping the journey family-friendly and enjoyable.

Day 5 – Atal Tunnel Drive & Scenic Excursion

Enjoy one of the most impressive road experiences in the region with a scenic drive through the Atal Tunnel side. This day is ideal for guests who enjoy mountain roads, fresh weather, and the premium feeling of a well-planned Himachal holiday. The changing views, tunnel route, and surrounding landscapes create a satisfying and complete travel experience.

Day 6 – Departure with Beautiful Mountain Memories

After breakfast, check out and prepare for your departure with unforgettable memories of Manali’s valleys, mountain roads, and refreshing atmosphere. This package is designed to leave guests feeling relaxed, satisfied, and ready to return again for a longer Himalayan holiday in the future.
