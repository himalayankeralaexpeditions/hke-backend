Day-wise itinerary

A smooth holiday progression from city arrival to hills and backwaters, planned to keep the experience scenic, restful, and worth booking.

Day 1 – Arrival at Kochi • Gentle Start to Your Kerala Journey

On arrival at Kochi, our representative will receive you and assist with your transfer. Depending on arrival time, you can relax and settle into the atmosphere of Kerala before the main journey begins. This day is intentionally kept comfortable so the trip starts in a smooth and stress-free way.

Day 2 – Kochi to Munnar • Scenic Hill Transfer

After breakfast, proceed towards Munnar through one of the most beautiful road journeys in Kerala. The route is known for its greenery, changing landscape, waterfalls, and hill-station freshness. As you reach Munnar, the cooler weather and tea-country atmosphere immediately transform the mood of the trip into a true holiday experience.

Day 3 – Munnar Sightseeing • Tea Hills, Views & Relaxed Exploration

Today is dedicated to the charm of Munnar. Explore major sightseeing points and enjoy the kind of scenic mountain atmosphere that makes this destination so popular with couples and families. The beauty of Munnar lies in its balance: peaceful views, refreshing air, and a slow premium holiday feel that allows guests to truly relax.

Day 4 – Munnar to Alleppey • Hills to Backwaters

After breakfast, continue towards Alleppey and witness a beautiful transition from misty hills to Kerala’s iconic backwater region. This shift in landscape gives the package a rich travel experience by combining two very different but equally memorable sides of Kerala. On arrival, settle in and enjoy the calm rhythm of the destination.

Day 5 – Alleppey Experience • Backwater Charm & Relaxation

Today you enjoy the timeless beauty of Alleppey, known for its peaceful waterways, houseboat atmosphere, and relaxed Kerala character. This is the kind of day guests often remember most, because it brings together calm scenery, quiet luxury, and the classic feeling of a Kerala holiday done properly.

Day 6 – Departure • Return with a Complete Kerala Story

After breakfast, check out and continue for your onward journey. By the end of the trip, you carry back not just photographs, but the feeling of having experienced Kerala in a balanced and elegant way, from hill stations to backwaters in one complete holiday package.
